Sample Technical Interview Questions

What are the four pillars of Object-Oriented Programming?

Encapsulation, abstraction, inheritance and polymorphism are the four pillars of OOP. They help organize code into reusable and easy-to-manage classes.

What is normalization in DBMS?

Normalization is the process of organizing a database to reduce redundancy and improve data integrity.

What is the difference between TCP and UDP?

TCP is connection-oriented and reliable. UDP is faster, connectionless and does not guarantee delivery.
